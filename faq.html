FAQ / ROBO SAHAYAK

Questions. Clear answers.

Answers based only on the specified RoboSahayak project.

What is RoboSahayak?

An assistive mobile robotic system designed to help elderly and disabled users fetch objects using a robotic arm.

Who is it designed to help?

Elderly people and people with disabilities who may have difficulty moving around or reaching objects.

Can it carry a person?

No. It is not designed to carry humans.

How is it controlled?

Glove Mode, Mobile Bluetooth Control Mode, Human Following Mode, Obstacle Avoidance Mode and Voice Control Mode.

What Bluetooth modules are used?

Two HC-05 modules; Nano is Master, UNO is Slave, communication baud rate is 38400.

What microcontrollers are used?

Arduino UNO SMD for the robot and Arduino Nano for the glove.

How does the glove work?

MPU6050 handles tilt/orientation and five flex sensors detect finger bending. Thumb position switches control mode.

How does human following work?

HC-SR04 measures distance: 0–39 cm backward, 40–42 cm stop, 43–100 cm forward.

How does obstacle avoidance work?

The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor detects obstacles to support avoidance behavior.

How is the arm controlled?

Five SG90 servos, 28BYJ-48 stepper, ULN2003 and PCA9685 form the arm system; specified glove fingers map to arm functions.

What happens after inactivity?

In Glove and Mobile Bluetooth modes, after 2 minutes the robot moves backward for 1 second.

What are the five modes?

Glove, Mobile Bluetooth Control, Human Following, Obstacle Avoidance and Voice Control.

Is the app available?

The website provides an editable place for the official download link.
