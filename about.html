About / ROBO SAHAYAK

The idea behind RoboSahayak.

Technology that helps • Robotics that cares

Assistive robotics with a clear purpose.

RoboSahayak combines mobile locomotion, a robotic arm, glove-based control, Bluetooth control, human following, obstacle avoidance and voice control.

Purpose

Make everyday object-fetching easier for people who may have difficulty moving around or reaching objects.

What it combines

Mobile robotic locomotion, robotic arm, glove-based control, mobile Bluetooth control, human following, obstacle avoidance and voice control.

Important boundary

RoboSahayak is designed to fetch objects and assist the user. It is NOT designed to carry humans.
