Robotic Arm / ROBO SAHAYAK

The RoboSahayak arm.

A multi-actuator object interaction system.

Five SG90 servos, a 28BYJ-48 stepper motor, ULN2003 driver and PCA9685 PWM driver form the arm system.

Arm architecture

Servo motors5 × SG90
Stepper motor28BYJ-48
Stepper driverULN2003
PWM driverPCA9685
